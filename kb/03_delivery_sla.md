# Delivery SLAs

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days to metro cities and 4 to 7 business days to the rest of India. Express delivery arrives on the next business day for eligible pin codes when the order is placed before 2 PM. An order is flagged as a delayed shipment once it misses the upper limit of its promised delivery SLA. Customers with a delayed shipment are notified proactively by SMS and email.
