# Warranty Terms by Category

Electronics such as hair dryers, straighteners and trimmers carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date, serviced at the brand's authorised service centres with the Nykaa invoice as proof of purchase. Home appliances carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Apparel, Footwear and Beauty products carry no warranty, but a manufacturing defect reported inside the return window is handled as a return or replacement. The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage or misuse.
