# Loyalty Points Redemption Policy

Customers earn Nykaa reward points on every delivered order at 1 point per Rs 100 spent, with higher earning rates for premium-tier members. One reward point is worth Rs 1, and points can pay for up to 50% of an order's value on orders of at least Rs 500. Points are credited only after the order's return window has closed and are reversed if the order is returned. Reward points expire 12 months after they are credited.
