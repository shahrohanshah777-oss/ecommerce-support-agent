# Return Window by Product Category

Nykaa accepts returns within a category-specific window that is counted from the delivery date. Apparel and Footwear can be returned within 15 days, Home products within 10 days, and Electronics within 7 days. Beauty products can be returned within 7 days only if the seal is unbroken and the product is unused, because opened cosmetics cannot be resold for hygiene reasons. Every return must include the original tags, packaging and any free gifts, otherwise it may be rejected at quality check.
