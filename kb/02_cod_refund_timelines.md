# COD Refund Timelines

For Cash-on-Delivery orders, a refund is issued only after the returned item passes quality check at the Nykaa warehouse. The customer can take the refund as Nykaa Wallet credit, which arrives within 24 hours of the quality check, or as a bank transfer by NEFT or IMPS, which arrives within 5 to 7 business days. Cash is never refunded at the doorstep. If the bank details are wrong the transfer fails, and it is retried once the customer updates the details, which restarts the 5 to 7 business day timeline.
