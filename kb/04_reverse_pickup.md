# Reverse-Pickup Eligibility

Reverse pickup is the free doorstep collection of an item that a customer is returning. It is available only while the order is inside its return window and only for pin codes served by Nykaa's reverse-logistics partners. Customers in non-serviceable pin codes must self-ship the item and receive up to Rs 150 in their Nykaa Wallet as a shipping reimbursement. Non-returnable items, such as opened Beauty products and innerwear, are not eligible for reverse pickup. The courier makes two pickup attempts, and if both fail the return request is cancelled automatically.
