# International Shipping Restrictions

Nykaa currently delivers only to addresses within India, and international addresses cannot be saved at checkout. Customers living abroad can order for delivery to an Indian address, but they must pay with an Indian-issued card, UPI or Nykaa Wallet. Within India, flammable items such as aerosols, nail polish removers and perfumes above 100 ml cannot travel by air, so they are sent by surface transport, which can add 2 to 3 days to the delivery SLA.
