# Damaged-Item Claim Process

If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what was ordered, the customer must raise a claim from the order details page within 48 hours of delivery. The claim must include clear photos of the item, the outer box and the shipping label, and an unboxing video is strongly recommended for Electronics. Valid claims are resolved within 3 business days with a free replacement or a full refund, even for Beauty products that would otherwise be non-returnable. Claims raised after 48 hours are treated as regular returns under the normal return window.
