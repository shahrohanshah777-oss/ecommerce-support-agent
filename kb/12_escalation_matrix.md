# Customer-Support Escalation Matrix

Level 1 is the chat and email support team, which aims to resolve every ticket within 24 hours. A ticket is escalated to Level 2, the senior resolution desk, if it is still unresolved after 48 hours or if it concerns a delayed shipment worth more than Rs 5,000, and Level 2 responds within 24 hours. Level 2 tickets unresolved after 72 hours, and any complaint about a double debit or suspected fraud, go to Level 3, the grievance officer, who must respond within 7 days. A customer can ask for escalation at any time by replying ESCALATE to their ticket email.
